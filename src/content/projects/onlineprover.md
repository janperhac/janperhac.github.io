OnlineProver supports learning natural deduction in propositional and first-order logic. It grew out of a collaboration between TUKE and the University of Oslo under initiative **FBR-PDI-025**, supported by EEA and Norway Grants.

I served as the TUKE principal investigator. The collaboration explored proof visualization, teaching materials, and interactive feedback.
